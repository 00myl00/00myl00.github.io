Experiments & Research

Audience research · March–June 2026

Transmedia Storytelling and Audience Reception in Cyberpunk: Edgerunners

I studied how Steam reviews of Cyberpunk 2077 changed around the release of its animated adaptation. I cleaned the review data in Python, classified review content using bilingual keyword rules, and compared patterns using chi-square tests.

Recommendations increased by nearly 5% in the period studied. The paper examines how a standalone adaptation can give audiences a new way to engage with an existing fictional world.
