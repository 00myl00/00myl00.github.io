Experiments & Research

Audience research · March–June 2026

How Cyberpunk: Edgerunners Was Received by Players

I analyzed Steam reviews of Cyberpunk 2077 before and after the release of its animated adaptation. I cleaned the data in Python, categorized reviews using keywords in two languages, and compared the results with chi-square tests.

The share of positive recommendations rose over the period I studied. I explored how a standalone adaptation can give audiences a new way into an existing fictional world.
